Holiday Schedule 2026

Your client’s approved preference for your 2026 holiday schedule. Data refreshes every 15 minutes.

Schedule Status Guide

Holiday Premium (1.5×)

You are required to work and your client approved 1.5× your regular rate for the hours worked.

Regular Day

You are required to work at your regular rate.

Paid Time Off

You are not required to work, and your client approved full pay for your scheduled hours.

Unpaid Time Off

You are not required to work, and no make-up hours are required.

Unpaid + Make-Up Hours

You are not required to work, but your client requires you to make up the hours on a different day.

TBD

Your client has not yet confirmed their preference. No action is needed for now.

Your 2026 Holiday Schedule

Your personalized holiday schedule table is published as a Looker Studio report. Open it to review the status for each holiday and follow the guidance that applies to you.

Open Holiday Schedule (Looker Studio)

What Do I Need to Do?

If your status is Paid Holiday Off

Manual Reporting, RS Time Tracking Tool, or client-provided tool users: no action needed — this is documented automatically.

If your client did not provide a paid holiday, but you want the day paid

You may use your PTO credits to have the day paid. Submit your request through the PTO Filing tab in the RT Portal, and attach a screenshot of your Holiday Schedule result as supporting documentation.

If your client did not provide a paid holiday, and you do not want to use PTO credits

Manual Reporting or time-tracking-tool users: no action needed.
