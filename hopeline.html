Hopeline

Confidential mental health support for Rocket Station teammates.

Request a Hopeline Session

About

Hopeline is a confidential support service available to all Rocket Station Remote Teammates. It provides a safe space to speak with trained professionals about mental health, emotional well-being, or personal challenges you may be experiencing.

Through Hopeline, you can request a private session to discuss concerns such as:

  • Stress or burnout
  • Work-life balance challenges
  • Personal or family concerns
  • Emotional well-being

All conversations are confidential and handled with care by the Hopeline support team.

Book a Hopeline Session

If you would like to speak with someone from the Hopeline Team, you may request a confidential session through the Hopeline request form.

Once your request is submitted, a member of the Hopeline team will contact you to confirm your appointment and provide the details for your consultation.

This service is part of Rocket Station's commitment to supporting the mental health and well-being of our Remote Teammates.
