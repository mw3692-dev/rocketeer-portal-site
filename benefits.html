Rocketeer Healthline

Rocketeer Healthline is a telemedicine benefit provided by Rocket Station that allows teammates to consult with licensed doctors online for quick medical guidance. This service is separate from your HMO and is available to all team members, regardless of HMO eligibility.

Through the platform, you and your immediate dependents can connect with licensed doctors for:

  • Health consultations
  • Medical advice
  • Initial diagnosis and guidance
  • Referrals to specialists if needed

All consultations are confidential and conducted directly between you and the doctor.

Rocketeer Hopeline

Hopeline is a confidential support service available to all Rocket Station Remote Teammates. It provides a safe space to speak with trained professionals about mental health, emotional well-being, or personal challenges you may be experiencing.

Through Hopeline, you can request a private session to discuss concerns such as:

  • Stress or burnout
  • Work-life balance challenges
  • Personal or family concerns
  • Emotional well-being

All conversations are confidential and handled with care by the Hopeline support team.

HMO Healthcare

Rocket Station provides healthcare coverage through Avega HMO for eligible Remote Teammates.

This program allows teammates to access accredited hospitals, clinics, and doctors for medical consultations, procedures, and healthcare support.

Key eligibility highlights:

  • Eligible for the first free dependent after 6 months of tenure
  • Second free dependent after 1 year of tenure
  • Additional dependents may be enrolled through PF (professional fee) deduction

Once enrollment is approved, account activation typically takes 15–30 working days.

Avega Member Portal

The Avega Agora Portal and mobile app let you manage your healthcare benefits in one place:

  • Access your digital HMO card
  • Search accredited hospitals and doctors
  • Book teleconsultations 24/7
  • Request Letters of Authorization (LOA)
  • Submit reimbursement requests
  • Track your healthcare usage and benefits
