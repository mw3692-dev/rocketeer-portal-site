HMO Healthcare

Healthcare coverage available to eligible Rocket Station Remote Teammates and their dependents.

Access Avega PortalEnroll Dependents

About

Rocket Station provides healthcare coverage through Avega HMO for eligible Remote Teammates. This program allows teammates to access accredited hospitals, clinics, and doctors for medical consultations, procedures, and healthcare support.

Key eligibility highlights:

  • Remote Teammates: eligible for their first free dependent after 6 months of tenure
  • Second free dependent after 1 year of tenure
  • Additional dependents may be enrolled through PF (professional fee) deduction

Once enrollment is approved, account activation typically takes 15–30 working days.

Avega Member Portal

The Avega Agora Portal and mobile app allow you to manage your healthcare benefits in one place. Through the portal you can:

  • Access your digital HMO card
  • Search for accredited hospitals and doctors
  • Book teleconsultations 24/7
  • Request Letters of Authorization (LOA)
  • Submit reimbursement requests
  • Track your healthcare usage and benefits

Enroll your dependents

Your eligibility to enroll dependents under the Rocket Station HMO program depends on your tenure with the company. Free dependent enrollment becomes available as you reach specific tenure milestones.

Important: Dependent enrollment is not automatic. Once you become eligible, Contractor Services will send you an HMO Eligibility Email with the enrollment instructions and the Jotform link required to submit your dependent information.

1. After 6 months of tenure — first free dependent

When you reach six months of tenure, you become eligible to enroll your first free dependent. Contractor Services sends an HMO Eligibility Email with the Jotform link; complete the submission and attach the required documents in PDF format. Processing takes 15–30 working days.

2. After one year of tenure — second free dependent

When you reach one year of tenure, you become eligible for a second free dependent, following the same Jotform submission process. Processing takes 15–30 working days.

3. Additional dependents (with PF deduction)

To enroll more than your free dependents, notify the HMO POC via email. You'll sign an Authorization to Deduct document (via Echo signing, copying Finance) authorizing the courier-fee deduction, then proceed with the Jotform submission. Processing takes 15–30 working days.
